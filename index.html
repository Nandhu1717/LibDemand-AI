⚙️ Machine Learning Architecture Details

LibDemand AI utilizes a Random Forest Regression ensemble model consisting of 120 decision trees. Features are preprocessed using Scikit-Learn ColumnTransformer and OneHotEncoder to prevent data leakage during scaling and encoding.

Algorithm: Random Forest Regressor
Tree Estimators: 120 Trees
Train/Test Split: 80% Train (480 rows) / 20% Test (120 rows)
Threshold Classification: Percentile Quantiles
Low Demand: < 85 books (< 33rd quantile)
High Demand: > 160 books (> 66th quantile)

📖 LibDemand AI User Guide & System Overview

📌 Problem Statement & Solution

Libraries need to keep enough copies of popular books available while avoiding the accumulation of resources that few students borrow. Manual stock planning relies on retrospective records. LibDemand AI solves this by analyzing historical circulation indicators (book category, month, semester, student headcount, past borrowings, and past demand) using Random Forest Machine Learning to accurately forecast future book demand.

🏷️ Demand Classification Rules

  • Low Demand (< 85 books): Retain existing shelf stock; avoid ordering unnecessary duplicate copies.
  • Medium Demand (85 – 160 books): Monitor circulation frequency; ensure reference desk reserve is populated.
  • High Demand (> 160 books): 🚨 Priority Reorder with suppliers; activate short-term borrowing reserves and digital copy access.

🔮 Future Roadmap
